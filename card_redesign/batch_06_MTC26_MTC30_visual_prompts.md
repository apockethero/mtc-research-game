# Batch 06 Visual Prompts: MTC26-MTC30

## Purpose

This batch prepares visuals for ride comfort, traffic-stream capacity, suspension dynamics, battery-consumption testing and cornering safety. The images must show test or modelling contexts without inventing numerical results or field validation.

Global constraints for all images:

- Square 1:1 academic knowledge-card visual.
- Photorealistic editorial style.
- One or two main visual elements only.
- No text, labels, numbers, logos, brand marks or watermarks inside the image.
- Clean upper third preserved for later card layout.
- No charts, dashboards, measurements or visible numerical results inside the image.
- Test scenes must look controlled and credible, not sensational.

## MTC26: Passenger Ride Comfort

Source key: `Sharma2021_BiodynamicRideComfort3W`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a seated passenger ride-comfort test setup for a three-wheeled vehicle, with a seat rig or neutral test dummy connected to a tricycle cabin reference and vibration-test equipment. The image should communicate comfort as a measurable design criterion linked to suspension and road input, without showing numerical measurements. Use clean laboratory lighting, realistic mechanical fixtures and a restrained technical palette. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, charts, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The seat or passenger rig and tricycle-cabin reference are clear.
- The visual reads as comfort/vibration testing.
- It avoids numerical results and measurement overlays.

Reject if:

- It shows a crash, unsafe ride or dramatic vibration.
- It becomes a generic car-seat test unrelated to three-wheelers.
- It includes labels, charts or numbers.

Accept only if:

- Three-wheeled vehicles and surrounding traffic mix are visible.
- The scene suggests capacity/road-space interaction without chaos.
- It avoids Bishoftu-specific or location-specific claims.

Reject if:

- It includes road signs, city labels or statistical graphics.

## MTC30: Cornering Safety Design

Source key: `Blatnicky2023_DynamicAnalysisCorneringSafety`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show an unconventional three-wheeled electric vehicle on a controlled cornering test track, with suspension and steering components visually emphasized through camera angle rather than labels. The image should communicate model-informed cornering safety design, not real-world deployment proof. Use daylight or clean test-track lighting, realistic speed restraint and professional engineering documentation style. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, logos, skid marks, crash imagery, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The vehicle is clearly three-wheeled and in a controlled turn.
- Suspension or steering relevance is visible.
- The scene avoids crash or stunt framing.

Reject if:

- It shows racing, drifting or dangerous cornering.
- It presents model-based evidence as field validation.
- It includes labels, diagrams, numbers or logos.
