# Block 4 Redesign Plan: MTC16--MTC20

## Editorial Status

This block covers ergonomics, adaptive mobility, service quality, powertrain sizing and steering stability. MTC16 is verified through a DOI record, MTC18 is verified in the local bibliography through `IntroCon11`, and MTC17, MTC19 and MTC20 now use verified replacement sources.

## Block Function in the Article

Block 4 deepens the human-centered and design-oriented side of the deck. It connects vehicle geometry, user comfort, control mechanisms, and service quality. This is important because the article argues that cards can help users navigate both technical and human dimensions of the electric tricycle literature.

## MTC16

### Current Source Metadata

- Title: An Ergonomic Analysis on the New E-Traysikel
- Authors: N. R. San Juan, K. L. Abaa, D. J. Alto, E. Fernando Jr., B. Custodio
- Year: 2020
- Venue: Advances in Human Aspects of Transportation
- Current category: Proceedings
- Verified source key: `SanJuan2020_ErgonomicETraysikel`
- Source status: Verified through Crossref

### Proposed Category

Structure, ergonomics and safety.

### Evidence Type

Ergonomic assessment / anthropometric comparison.

### Core Idea

Ergonomic evaluation can reveal whether electric tricycle dimensions fit the bodies, postures and comfort needs of intended users.

### Main Contribution

The card should show that electric tricycle design must consider passenger and driver fit, not only propulsion or energy performance.

### Limitation / Caution

The card should report ergonomic findings as context-specific unless anthropometric and participant details are verified.

### Visual Direction

Realistic e-trike cabin with visible seat, handlebar and passenger area, shown in an ergonomic inspection setting.

### Final Image Prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: square visual element for an academic knowledge card. Create a realistic ergonomic inspection scene of an electric tricycle cabin. Show the seat, handlebar, passenger area and posture-related geometry clearly, without measurement labels. Clean workshop or transport lab, professional human-factors style, clean upper third for text overlay. No text, no logos, no labels, no numbers, no watermark.
