# Batch 01 Visual Prompts: MTC01-MTC05

## Purpose

This batch prepares the first five card visuals for controlled image generation. Each prompt is source-aligned and must be checked before any image is accepted into the pilot deck.

Global constraints for all images:

- Square 1:1 academic knowledge-card visual.
- Photorealistic editorial style.
- One or two main visual elements only.
- No text, labels, numbers, logos, brand marks or watermarks inside the image.
- Clean upper third preserved for later card layout.
- Realistic materials, scale and engineering context.
- No exaggerated futuristic design.

## MTC01: Reverse-Pedaling Brake

Source key: `Kulkarni2020_ReversePedalingBraking`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a close realistic view of a hybrid tricycle reverse-pedaling brake mechanism: one foot pedal, crank interface, brake linkage and part of the rear wheel assembly visible in a clean engineering workshop. The scene should communicate a simple mechanical braking interface for a small tricycle platform. Use natural workshop lighting, realistic metal and rubber materials, and a restrained technical palette. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, logos, diagrams, arrows, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The image clearly shows a pedal/brake linkage relationship.
- It does not imply a fully validated road-safety system.
- It does not include labels, arrows or diagram text.

Reject if:

- It looks like a bicycle-only part with no tricycle context.
- It invents a digital dashboard or futuristic braking system.
- It places written annotations inside the image.

## MTC02: Smart Solar Electric Tricycle

Source key: `Mohamed2016_SmartSolarElectricTricycle`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a compact smart solar electric tricycle prototype in a clean prototype bay, with a roof-mounted photovoltaic panel and a visible compact electric drivetrain or battery compartment. The vehicle should look like a practical small electric tricycle, not a luxury concept. Use clean studio or workshop lighting, realistic scale and restrained technical colors. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, logos, fingerprint scanners, phone screens, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The photovoltaic panel and electric-tricycle form are both visible.
- The design remains realistic and buildable.
- The image avoids fingerprint-access imagery.

Reject if:

- It centers on biometric access, phones or smart-home style elements.
- It looks like a four-wheel vehicle, scooter or generic solar car.
- It contains written labels or logos.

## MTC03: Agricultural Tricycle Prototype

Source key: `Han2012_AgriculturalTricycleVirtualPrototype`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a realistic agricultural tricycle prototype or physical mock-up on a rural-road test setup, with the driver position, three-wheel frame and road-surface context clearly visible. The image should suggest driver-vehicle-road interaction through physical composition, not through diagrams or text. Use natural daylight, credible agricultural engineering materials and a restrained rural-technical palette. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, maps, country flags, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The image shows a tricycle suited to agricultural or rural-road use.
- Driver, vehicle and road context are implied visually.
- It avoids unsupported geographic claims.

Reject if:

- It depicts Ghana, dry-soil claims or mounted implement findings.
- It becomes a farm tractor rather than a three-wheeled vehicle.
- It includes text, flags or location labels.

## MTC04: Solar-Conversion Performance

Source key: `Mohammed2023_ConversionSolarThreeWheeler`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a converted electric three-wheeler in a clean engineering test bay, with a visible onboard solar charging panel, battery compartment and compact electric drivetrain access panel. The scene should communicate conversion and performance evaluation of a petrol three-wheeler to electric power, not a futuristic concept. Use realistic workshop lighting, credible vehicle scale and restrained technical colors. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, logos, arrows, dashboards, energy graphics, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The converted three-wheeler, solar charging element and electric powertrain context are visible.
- The image reads as engineering evaluation, not product advertising.
- It remains distinct from MTC02 and MTC40 by emphasizing conversion/test-bay context.

Reject if:

- It shows a wind turbine, pinwheel or free-energy device.
- It looks like a generic solar car, four-wheeler or luxury concept.
- It contains text, labels, dashboards or infographic elements.

## MTC05: Elderly-Mobility Chassis

Source key: `Anon2024_TricycleChassisElderly`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a tricycle chassis designed for elderly mobility in a clean workshop: a stable low-step frame, accessible seat position and clear three-wheel support geometry. Focus on chassis access, stability and human-centered design, with one older adult silhouette or neutral mannequin only if useful for scale. Use realistic metal tubing, workshop lighting and restrained colors. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, cargo boxes, exposed suspension springs as the main element, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The low-step accessible chassis is the central visual idea.
- Stability and access are visible without exaggerated medical imagery.
- It avoids cargo and spring claims.

Reject if:

- It turns into a cargo tricycle or delivery vehicle.
- It emphasizes suspension springs instead of chassis access.
- It includes text, labels or brand marks.
