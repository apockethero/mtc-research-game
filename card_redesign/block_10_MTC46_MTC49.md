## Editorial Status

This final block covers regulatory classification, microenterprise performance, battery/motor sizing and real-world electric two- and three-wheeler performance. MTC46 is verified through `Yahya2021_MotorizedPedicabsIndonesia`, MTC47 is verified through `Awuni2023_MotorTricycleMSME`, MTC48 is locally verified through `Wanene2022_BatteryMotorSizing`, and MTC49 is verified through `Majumdar2022_E2E3W`.

## Block Function in the Article

Block 10 closes the deck by connecting technical cards with social, regulatory and strategic deployment questions. It is especially useful for the pilot because it tests whether the card format can help readers move from engineering details to broader systems reasoning without treating every source as the same kind of evidence.
