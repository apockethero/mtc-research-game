# Batch 03 Visual Prompts: MTC11-MTC15

## Purpose

This batch prepares card visuals for hybrid rickshaw energy benchmarking, accessibility, physical activity, public acceptance and operational efficiency. The prompts keep the evidence level visible: supporting or applied sources must not be visually framed as large-scale validated deployment.

Global constraints for all images:

- Square 1:1 academic knowledge-card visual.
- Photorealistic editorial style.
- One or two main visual elements only.
- No text, labels, numbers, logos, brand marks or watermarks inside the image.
- Clean upper third preserved for later card layout.
- Respectful representation of users and communities.
- No exaggerated futuristic vehicle design.

## MTC11: Hybrid Rickshaw Fuel-Economy Benchmark

Source key: `Asghar2018_HybridRickshawFuelEconomy`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a hybrid electric three-wheeler rickshaw powertrain evaluation setup in a clean lab or workshop: compact engine or generator module, electric motor, battery pack and drivetrain connection visible near a three-wheeled chassis section. The scene should communicate benchmark fuel-economy modelling for a hybrid rickshaw, not road-tested universal performance. Use realistic components, neutral engineering lighting and restrained colors. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, logos, dashboards, charts, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- Hybrid three-wheeler rickshaw powertrain components are visible.
- It reads as an energy benchmark or evaluation setup.
- It remains distinct from battery-electric sizing cards.

Reject if:

- It becomes a human-powered bicycle, go-kart or racing vehicle.
- It implies measured field fuel economy for all rickshaws.
- It includes text, dashboards, charts or logos.

## MTC12: Accessible Electric Tricycle

Source key: `Comp52`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show an accessible electric tricycle prototype designed for a physically challenged user, with a stable three-wheel frame, low entry area, supportive seat and clear control module. The scene should focus on inclusive mobility, stability and affordability through practical design. Use a respectful workshop or quiet outdoor setting, realistic materials and a restrained technical palette. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, disability symbols, hospital imagery, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- Stability, low entry and control access are clear.
- The scene is respectful and non-stereotyped.
- It looks like an applied prototype, not a luxury product.

Reject if:

- It uses medical or pity-based imagery.
- It shows a wheelchair alone instead of an electric tricycle.
- It contains labels, icons or logos.

## MTC13: Outdoor E-Trike Cycling

Source key: `Bulthuis2021_OutdoorETrikeCycling`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show one rider using an outdoor e-trike on a quiet park path, with stable posture, pedals and three-wheel configuration visible. The image should communicate low-intensity physical activity and controlled mobility in a calm setting. Use natural daylight, realistic clothing and a documentary style. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, logos, medical symbols, fitness metrics, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The vehicle is clearly an e-trike.
- The rider posture suggests gentle, low-intensity activity.
- The image avoids clinical or competitive framing.

Reject if:

- It shows racing, intense exercise or unsafe speed.
- It depicts a two-wheel bicycle or scooter.
- It adds fitness-dashboard graphics or labels.

## MTC14: E-Trike Public Acceptance

Source key: `Comp30`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show an electric tricycle stopped at a clean public transport boarding area in a modern urban district, with one or two passengers waiting calmly as secondary elements. The image should communicate service experience, perceived convenience and safe boarding conditions. Use urban daylight, realistic street materials and a professional documentary style. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, route signs, numbers, logos, advertising, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The public transport stop and boarding context are clear.
- The scene remains calm, clean and non-promotional.
- It avoids implying acceptance results beyond one urban context.

Reject if:

- It shows a crowd, protest or campaign advertisement.
- It contains route names, signs, slogans or logos.
- It presents the vehicle as a proven citywide solution.

## MTC15: E-Trike Operational Efficiency

Source key: `Nacion2022_OperationalEfficiencyETrikePUB`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a small public transport terminal comparison scene with one electric tricycle and one conventional motorized tricycle positioned near a simple queue area. The visual should suggest operational comparison, service flow and terminal conditions without showing charts or written metrics. Use clean daylight, limited background activity and realistic public-utility vehicle proportions. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, logos, route boards, charts, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- Both electric and conventional three-wheeled vehicles are visible.
- The queue or terminal condition is simple and readable.
- The image does not claim broad system superiority.

Reject if:

- It includes performance charts, route labels or fleet statistics.
- It becomes a crowded street scene with no operational comparison.
- It shows unrelated buses, cars or motorcycles as the main subject.
