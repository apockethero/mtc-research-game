# Block 1 Redesign Plan: MTC01--MTC05

## Editorial Status

This block starts the redesign of the complete 49-card deck. MTC01 has a verified DOI record, and MTC02, MTC03, MTC04 and MTC05 now use verified replacement sources. Final card text must preserve those replacement boundaries.

## Block Function in the Article

Block 1 introduces early technical and applied design problems in the MTC deck:

- braking and safety;
- renewable-energy integration and conversion;
- agricultural or rural adaptation;
- rural/agricultural design adaptation;
- chassis and suspension design.

This block helps show that the state-of-the-art corpus is not limited to urban delivery or public transport. It includes early engineering concepts, rural adaptation and accessibility-oriented chassis design that must be clearly labelled by evidence strength.
