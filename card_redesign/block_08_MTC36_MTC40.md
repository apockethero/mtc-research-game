## Editorial Status

This block covers advanced stability control, smart industrial logistics, accessible mobility and solar-powered tricycle prototypes. MTC36 has been reassigned to `RodriguezLicea2018_DeltaTricycleRolloverRisk` to avoid duplication with MTC31. MTC37 is verified through `Kovac2024_SmartThreeWheeledUnit`. ## Block Function in the Article

Block 8 helps the deck show breadth: mathematical safety control, industrial application, inclusive mobility and renewable-energy prototypes. For the article, this block is valuable because it tests whether the card method can translate highly different evidence types into a coherent state-of-the-art interface without flattening their methodological differences.

## MTC36

### Current Source Metadata

- Title: The Rollover Risk in Delta Tricycles: A New Rollover Index and Its Robust Mitigation by Rear Differential Braking
- Authors: M. A. Rodriguez Licea, E. A. Vazquez Rodriguez, F. J. Perez Pinal, J. Prado Olivares
- Year: 2018
- Venue: Mathematical Problems in Engineering
- Current category: Q2
- Verified source key: `RodriguezLicea2018_DeltaTricycleRolloverRisk`
- Source status: Verified replacement source

### Proposed Category

Structure, ergonomics and safety.

### Evidence Type

Rollover index / robust control simulation.

### Core Idea

Delta tricycles require a dedicated rollover-risk index because their stability limits differ from four-wheeled vehicles, and rear differential braking can mitigate rollover risk in simulation.

### Main Contribution

The card should introduce rollover risk as a measurable design and control problem for delta tricycles, before later cards present newer controller implementations.

### Limitation / Caution

The final card must state that the evidence is based on modelling and numerical simulation, not field deployment.

### Visual Direction

Delta-type tricycle in a controlled engineering test environment, emphasizing rollover-risk measurement and rear differential braking without crash imagery.

### Final Image Prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: square visual element for an academic knowledge card. Create a realistic delta-type three-wheeled vehicle on a controlled stability test rig. Emphasize rear differential braking, suspension response and stability instrumentation, without crash, damage or dramatic motion. Professional engineering research style, clean upper third for text overlay. No text, no logos, no labels, no numbers, no watermark.
