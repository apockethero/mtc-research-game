# Block 3 Redesign Plan: MTC11--MTC15

## Editorial Status

This block focuses on hybrid rickshaw energy benchmarking, inclusive electric mobility, physical activity, public transport acceptance and operational efficiency. MTC12 and MTC14 are verified through local mappings, MTC13 and MTC15 have verified DOI records, and MTC11 now uses a stronger verified replacement source.

## Block Function in the Article

Block 3 connects the technical corpus with users, accessibility and public-service operation. It is important for the article because it shows that the card deck is not only about components or engineering systems, but also about human use, acceptance and service performance.
