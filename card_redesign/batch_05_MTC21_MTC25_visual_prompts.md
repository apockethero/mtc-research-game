# Batch 05 Visual Prompts: MTC21-MTC25

## Purpose

This batch prepares visuals for tilting stability, supporting lightweight-frame evidence, human-powered hybrid tricycle design, last-mile cargo application and rollover stability analysis. Safety visuals must remain technical and non-sensational.

Global constraints for all images:

- Square 1:1 academic knowledge-card visual.
- Photorealistic editorial style.
- One or two main visual elements only.
- No text, labels, numbers, logos, brand marks or watermarks inside the image.
- Clean upper third preserved for later card layout.
- No crash scenes, damage scenes or dramatic failure imagery.
- Evidence-transfer limits must be respected visually.

## MTC21: Tuneable Tilting Trike

Source key: `Dressel2022_TiltingTrikeTuneableStability`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a tilting trike prototype in a controlled low-speed turn on a clean test path, with the leaning mechanism and stable three-wheel geometry visible. The image should communicate rider-tuneable handling and stability as a prototype safety-design idea, not a proven universal safety solution. Use natural daylight, realistic mechanical components and a professional safety-testing style. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, logos, motion graphics, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The trike is visibly leaning in a controlled way.
- Three-wheel geometry remains clear.
- The image does not imply high-speed racing.

Reject if:

- It shows a two-wheel bicycle or motorcycle.
- It uses dramatic motion blur or stunt imagery.
- It includes labels, logos or numbers.

## MTC22: Narrow Three-Wheeler Dynamics Review

Source key: `Dandiwala2022_NarrowThreeWheeledEVReview`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a narrow electric three-wheeled vehicle on a dynamics test platform, with tilt/roll posture, suspension details and instrumented wheel contact visible, but without readable instruments. The scene should communicate vehicle dynamics and active rollover-stability concerns at review level, not a single crash test. Use realistic lab lighting, credible vehicle geometry and restrained safety colors. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, colored diagrams, crash imagery, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The image clearly shows a narrow electric three-wheeler in a dynamics or stability evaluation context.
- The vehicle geometry and roll/tilt context are plausible.
- It reads as review-level safety/dynamics evidence.

Reject if:

- It shows an accident, rollover crash or damaged vehicle.
- It becomes an abstract simulation graphic or colored stress map.
- It includes text, labels, numbers or readable instruments.

## MTC23: Tilting Three-Wheeler Stability Control

Source key: `Chatterjee2019_NarrowTiltingThreeWheeledStability`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a narrow tilting electric three-wheeler on a controlled test platform, with visible steering actuator, suspension linkage and compact controller hardware near the chassis. The image should communicate integrated stability control for a tilting three-wheeled EV, not generic suspension fabrication. Use credible engineering-lab lighting, realistic components and restrained colors. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, dashboards, colored control diagrams, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- Tilting three-wheeler geometry, steering/suspension linkage and controller context are visible.
- It is visually distinct from MTC21 by emphasizing controlled stability hardware rather than rider-tuneable tilt.
- It reads as model/control evidence, not field deployment.

Reject if:

- It looks like a human-powered efficycle or racing prototype.
- It shows a crash, stunt or unsafe public-road maneuver.
- It contains labels, numbers, diagrams or readable screens.

## MTC25: Rollover Stability Analysis

Source key: `Endrasari2023_RolloverStabilityE3W`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a three-wheeled electric vehicle on a controlled tilt platform for rollover stability testing in a safety lab. The vehicle, tilt platform and protective test environment should be visible, with no crash, no damage and no dramatic failure. The image should communicate stability analysis as a safety-design tool. Use professional engineering lighting and realistic test equipment. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, logos, warning graphics, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- Vehicle and tilt platform are both visible.
- It looks like a controlled safety test.
- It avoids crash or accident imagery.

Reject if:

- It shows a rollover accident or damaged vehicle.
- It uses dramatic warning graphics or labels.
- It presents proceedings evidence as field deployment validation.
