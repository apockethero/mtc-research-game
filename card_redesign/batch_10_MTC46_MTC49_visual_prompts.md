# Batch 10 Visual Prompts: MTC46-MTC49

## Purpose

This batch closes the visual prompt set for the 49-card MTC deck. It covers legal classification, MSME logistics, locally verified battery/motor sizing evidence and real-world electric two- and three-wheeler performance evidence.

Global constraints for all images:

- Square 1:1 academic knowledge-card visual.
- Photorealistic editorial style.
- One or two main visual elements only.
- No text, labels, numbers, logos, brand marks or watermarks inside the image.
- Clean upper third preserved for later card layout.
- No readable signs, flags, license plates, dashboards or jurisdiction names.
- Context-specific evidence must not be shown as universal policy, economic or technical proof.

## MTC46: Motorized Pedicabs in Law

Source key: `Yahya2021_MotorizedPedicabsIndonesia`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a motorized pedicab in an Indonesian-style urban street context, with regulatory or public-road governance implied through a neutral inspection scene, road edge or civic setting, but without readable signage. The image should communicate that legal classification shapes permits, safety and operating rights. Use documentary daylight, realistic street materials and a restrained policy-oriented composition. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, flags, license plates, government logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The vehicle reads as a motorized pedicab or three-wheeled public-road vehicle.
- Legal/governance context is implied without readable signage.
- The scene remains jurisdiction-specific without naming or generalizing.

Reject if:

- It includes flags, official seals, license plates or readable signs.
- It turns the legal issue into a generic traffic-police scene.
- It implies that Indonesian findings apply universally.

## MTC47: Motor Tricycles and MSMEs

Source key: `Awuni2023_MotorTricycleMSME`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a motor tricycle supporting micro and small manufacturing logistics in a constrained urban or peri-urban environment, with small business goods or raw materials as the secondary element. The image should communicate three-wheeled mobility as an economic and supply-chain resource under precarious transport conditions. Use documentary daylight, realistic local business context and respectful non-promotional framing. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, flags, shop names, license plates, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The motor tricycle and small-business goods movement are clear.
- The context suggests constrained local logistics.
- It preserves the study context without turning it into a universal economic claim.

Reject if:

- It shows generic parcel delivery unrelated to MSMEs.
- It includes readable shop signs, plates or branding.
- It stereotypes the setting or overdramatizes poverty.

## MTC48: Battery and Motor Sizing

Source key: `Wanene2022_BatteryMotorSizing`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show an electric tricycle design bench with motor, battery pack, drivetrain connection and simple measuring tools arranged in a clean engineering workspace. The image should communicate a procedural sizing workflow based on mass, speed, acceleration and operating requirements, without showing formulas or numerical values. Use realistic components, neutral lab lighting and a restrained technical palette. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, equations, charts, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- Motor, battery and drivetrain relation are visible.
- It reads as a sizing/design workflow, not product advertising.
- It does not imply external index validation beyond the local proceedings source.

Reject if:

- It contains formulas, numbers or readable measuring screens.
- It shows only a generic battery pack with no tricycle context.
- It presents the method as externally validated field evidence.

## MTC49: Real-World E2W/E3W Performance

Source key: `Majumdar2022_E2E3W`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show electric two- and three-wheelers in a realistic heterogeneous urban traffic setting, with an electric auto-rickshaw or e-trike as the main subject and a small electric two-wheeler as secondary context. The scene should communicate real-world performance under mixed traffic conditions, not a laboratory prototype. Use documentary daylight, credible road scale and restrained colors. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, license plates, signs, flags, dashboards, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The electric three-wheeler is the main subject and heterogeneous traffic context is visible.
- The scene reads as field-performance context, not a prototype lab image.
- It does not imply that one case study applies universally.

Reject if:

- It uses abstract AI imagery, glowing graphics or lab-only components.
- It shows readable signs, plates, dashboards, text or numbers.
- It becomes generic traffic without an electric two- or three-wheeler focus.
