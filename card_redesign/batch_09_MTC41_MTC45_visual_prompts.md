# Batch 09 Visual Prompts: MTC41-MTC45

## Purpose

This batch prepares visuals for hybrid accessible tricycle design, auto-rickshaw maintenance, lightweight frame evaluation, safety verification and three-wheeler motor-drive evidence. The images must preserve context and evidence level, especially for MTC42 and MTC45.

Global constraints for all images:

- Square 1:1 academic knowledge-card visual.
- Photorealistic editorial style.
- One or two main visual elements only.
- No text, labels, numbers, logos, brand marks or watermarks inside the image.
- Clean upper third preserved for later card layout.
- No medicalized accessibility imagery, sensational safety scenes or product-advertising style.
- Supporting evidence must not be visually presented as direct tricycle field testing.

## MTC41: Hybrid Electrical Tricycle

Source key: `Swapna2020_HybridElectricalTricycle`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a hybrid/electric accessible tricycle prototype with stable frame, clear user controls, visible battery placement and simple charging or solar-support element in a clean workshop. The image should communicate the relationship between accessibility, propulsion and low-cost design, without claiming user-tested performance. Use realistic prototype materials, respectful framing and a restrained technical palette. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, disability icons, hospital imagery, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- Stable frame, controls and battery placement are visible.
- Accessibility is represented through design features, not stereotypes.
- It reads as prototype/design evidence.

Reject if:

- It duplicates MTC39 as a solar-autonomy card.
- It uses medical or pity-based imagery.
- It implies field-tested user outcomes without evidence.

## MTC42: Auto-Rickshaw Maintenance

Source key: `OwusuAnsah2022_AutoRickshawMaintenance`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a mechanic inspecting an auto-rickshaw or motor tricycle drivetrain, wheel or suspension component in a clean but realistic repair setting, with tools and parts visible as secondary elements. The image should communicate repair and servicing capacity as part of sustainable deployment in a local entrepreneurship context. Use documentary workshop lighting, realistic materials and a respectful non-staged composition. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, shop names, logos, license plates, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- Maintenance or servicing activity is clear.
- The vehicle is recognizably auto-rickshaw or motor tricycle related.
- The image does not claim universal fleet reliability.

Reject if:

- It becomes a generic car repair scene.
- It includes readable shop names, plates or logos.
- It portrays unsafe or exploitative working conditions as spectacle.

## MTC43: Lightweight Frame Design

Source key: `Pan2021_LightweightTricycleFrame`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show an electric tricycle frame under dynamic driving-load evaluation on an engineering lab stand, with chassis members, wheel mounts and realistic load fixtures visible. The image should communicate the trade-off between lightweight design and structural stress under realistic driving conditions, without showing exact weight-reduction numbers. Use clean lab lighting, realistic metal frame materials and a restrained technical palette. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, colored stress maps, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The frame and dynamic-load evaluation context are visible.
- It differs from MTC06 static loading by suggesting dynamic driving loads.
- It avoids numerical weight-reduction claims.

Reject if:

- It shows only a static bare frame with no dynamic-load context.
- It includes stress-map colors, labels or numbers.
- It implies final road certification.

## MTC44: Safety Verification

Source key: `Mongkonlerdmanee2023_SafetyVerification`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show an electric tuk-tuk or electric tricycle undergoing controlled safety verification in a test facility, with braking inspection and electrical safety check equipment visible as the main elements. The image should communicate that safety is technical and contextual, shaped by standards, operating environment and use conditions. Use professional test-facility lighting, realistic vehicle scale and restrained safety colors. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, national flags, logos, crash imagery, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- Braking or electrical safety inspection is clear.
- The test facility context is controlled and credible.
- It does not turn one national context into a universal standard.

Reject if:

- It shows an accident, fire or damaged vehicle.
- It includes readable standards, country names or flags.
- It looks like a generic vehicle showroom.

## MTC45: PMBLDC Motor for Three-Wheeler EV

Source key: `Sandeep2019_PMBLDCMotorThreeWheeler`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a PMBLDC motor design setup for a three-wheeler EV: compact motor, stator/rotor assembly, controller and drivetrain coupling arranged beside a partial three-wheeled chassis detail. The image should communicate component design for three-wheeler application, not a universal best motor claim. Use realistic components, neutral lab lighting and restrained technical colors. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, product packaging, brand marks, logos, watermarks or advertising style.
```

Accept only if:

- PMBLDC motor, controller and drivetrain relation are visible.
- It reads as three-wheeler EV component design evidence.
- It stays distinct from MTC19 and MTC48 by focusing on motor design.

Reject if:

- It shows only an e-bike with no three-wheeler context.
- It includes product branding or labels.
- It suggests one universal best motor-drive configuration.
