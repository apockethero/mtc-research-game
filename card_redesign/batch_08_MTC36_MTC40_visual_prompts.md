# Batch 08 Visual Prompts: MTC36-MTC40

## Purpose

This batch prepares visuals for delta-tricycle rollover-risk modelling, smart industrial logistics, accessible electric-tricycle prototyping and solar-assisted tricycle design. MTC36 must be visually distinct from MTC31 and MTC34: it represents rollover-risk modelling, not a repeated controller implementation.

Global constraints for all images:

- Square 1:1 academic knowledge-card visual.
- Photorealistic editorial style.
- One or two main visual elements only.
- No text, labels, numbers, logos, brand marks or watermarks inside the image.
- Clean upper third preserved for later card layout.
- No crash scenes, exaggerated futuristic vehicles or promotional product styling.
- Prototype and modelling evidence must not be shown as full field validation.

## MTC36: Delta-Tricycle Rollover Risk

Source key: `RodriguezLicea2018_DeltaTricycleRolloverRisk`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a delta-type three-wheeled vehicle in a controlled engineering test environment, positioned on a stable platform or test lane that suggests rollover-risk measurement and modelling. The image should communicate rollover risk as a measurable design/control problem for delta tricycles, without duplicating rear differential braking hardware as the main focus. Use realistic lab or test-track lighting, credible vehicle geometry and restrained technical colors. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, graphs, rear-braking emphasis, logos, crash imagery, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The delta-tricycle geometry is clear.
- The scene reads as modelling or controlled risk measurement.
- It is distinct from MTC31 rear differential braking and MTC34 front steering.

Reject if:

- It focuses mainly on rear differential braking hardware.
- It shows active front steering as the main mechanism.
- It depicts a crash, rollover accident or field deployment.

## MTC37: Smart Three-Wheeled Industrial Unit

Source key: `Kovac2024_SmartThreeWheeledUnit`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show a smart electric three-wheeled logistics unit moving light materials inside a clean manufacturing facility, with shelves, components or containers as subtle secondary context. The image should communicate compact indoor material movement and intelligent industrial mobility, not public transport. Use clean factory lighting, realistic industrial floor materials and a restrained professional palette. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, factory logos, warning signs, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- The three-wheeled industrial unit is clearly visible.
- The setting is indoor manufacturing or logistics.
- It does not imply general productivity gains beyond the demonstrated context.

Reject if:

- It looks like a public e-trike or passenger vehicle.
- It includes readable factory signs, labels or logos.
- It becomes a futuristic warehouse robot unrelated to three-wheelers.

Source key: `Yelure2019_ElectricTricycleHandicapped`

Prompt:

```text
Create a square photorealistic editorial image for an academic knowledge card. Show an accessible electric tricycle prototype with stable three-wheel frame, simple control layout, visible battery placement and accessible seating. The scene should communicate mechanical and electrical design for mobility limitations while remaining respectful and non-stereotyped. Use a clean workshop or quiet outdoor prototype setting, realistic materials and restrained technical colors. Leave the upper third visually clean for later text overlay. Do not include text, labels, numbers, disability icons, hospital imagery, logos, watermarks or brand marks.
```

Accept only if:

- Stable frame, controls and electric components are visible.

Reject if:

- It shows a wheelchair alone instead of an electric tricycle.
- It implies user testing or field validation not documented by the source.
